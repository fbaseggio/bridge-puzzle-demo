About

DeepSqueeze is a work in progress. Features, explanations, and classifications are still being developed, and you may encounter errors or incomplete material. Feedback and corrections are welcome.

DeepSqueeze explores contract-bridge squeezes through interactive diagrams, explanations, and computational research. It is developed and maintained by Franco Baseggio, based in the United States.
