Sources & credits

DeepSqueeze has many inspirations. This is a partial list of books and websites that have informed the project.

Websites

Richard Pavlicek — For Bridge Players. Lessons, problems, and analysis covering many aspects of bridge. Selected examples can be opened in Movie using their Pav IDs, with links to the original pages. You can also browse our Pav positions index.

John R. Lee — Bridge wiki. An extensive exploration of squeeze positions and their classification. Our JRL positions index lets you explore selected examples in Movie and follow links to their original sections.

Hugh Darwen — Double Dummy Corner. A collection of double-dummy bridge problems, with historical archives, solving competitions, and articles on card play.
