Privacy

DeepSqueeze is a personal project maintained by Franco Baseggio in the United States.

Using the site. Bridge-position analysis runs in your browser. Positions and play sequences can appear in page URLs, so they may be included in browser history, shared links, and hosting logs.

Cookies and browser storage. DeepSqueeze does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses temporary browser storage to cache information during your visit.

Hosting. DeepSqueeze is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and requests, to deliver and secure the site.

Email. Messages sent to contact@deepsqueeze.ai are forwarded through Cloudflare to my personal Gmail account. I do not sell email addresses. Emailing me will not add you to a mailing list.

Other websites. Links to external websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

For privacy questions, contact contact@deepsqueeze.ai.
